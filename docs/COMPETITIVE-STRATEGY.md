## 1. Positioning — the moat is the middle

Three live strategic plays in the NL boutique-PT market:

| | Positioning | Pricing | Lead capture | City |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Upshape** | Premium-exclusive, narrow ("new standard") | **Hidden** | Phone-gated | Den Haag |
| **Power Factory** | Accessible-fun, 5-product breadth | Transparent, tiered | Form-routed | Rotterdam |
| **SculptClub** | **Boutique-private, dual-funnel** | **Transparent** | WhatsApp-async | Amsterdam (Jordaan) |

**The wedge:** SculptClub sits *between* Upshape (too exclusive — researchers who want prices upfront bounce) and Power Factory (too diffuse — 5 products overwhelm). Buyers who research + want to know the price find SculptClub the right balance. **Don't drift toward either pole.**

## 2. The 5 moats (real, but were undiscovered pre-audit)

1. **Transparent pricing** — every Jordaan rival (U.P., Omnia, Staetsgeheim, Studio Performance Boost) HIDES prices. SculptClub shows them. → shipped as the homepage wedge (C) + /over-ons "Transparant" principle (M).
2. **0%-commission / independent-trainer model** — trainers keep 100%, set own rates. → "Trainer-eerst" principle (M); never flatten into a house "method" (R names the *format*, not a method).
3. **Match-quiz funnel** — 3 questions × 30 sec; tighter than rivals' contact forms.
4. **Mobile-perfection discipline** — 89% mobile traffic; rivals aren't audited to this bar.
5. **Dual-funnel** — uniquely serves BOTH consumers (PT) AND trainers-as-customers (studio rental). Power Factory has 5 funnels but all consumer/corporate; SculptClub's ZZP-trainer rental is a distinct, high-LTV channel (each trainer brings a client roster).

## 3. THE #1 PRIORITY (everything else is secondary)

**Review count.** SculptClub ~20 Google reviews vs U.P. 719, The Gym Amsterdam 126. This — not on-site copy — is why SculptClub is invisible in the local SERP despite a *better-built site*. Review count + GBP completeness are the #1+#2 local-pack ranking signals.

**The fix (operator-side, on the PromptPrio board):**
- **GBP optimization** — 20+ photos, services+prices, products, 5 seeded Q&A, weekly posts, categories.
- **Review-request flow** — post-session WhatsApp asking for a Google review (trainer buy-in via the broadcast). Target: 50 reviews in 90 days.

No amount of on-site work substitutes for this. The 17 on-site ships *amplify* it.

## 5. Pricing-elasticity flag (strategic, not yet acted)

Premium-Jordaan PT runs **€70–120/session** (Cocoon €90 · The Gym €70–95 · U.P. €3–5K packages). SculptClub's "vanaf €45" sits at the **budget-freelance floor (€40–60)** → may *undersell* the premium-boutique positioning. BUT trainers set their own rates (0% model), so it's a **framing** choice, not a price change. Option to consider: show a "€45–€80" range to signal the premium tier without changing any trainer's rate. (Per-trainer real rates — Alex €69, etc. — already on their pages.)
