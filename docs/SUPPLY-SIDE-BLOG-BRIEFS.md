## Cross-cutting writing guidelines (ALL 7 posts)

### Voice + register
- **Natural Dutch only.** Geen "1-op-1 training sessions" — wel "1-op-1 trainingen" of "persoonlijke begeleiding".
- Conversational + direct. Geen "It is important to note that..." style. Wel: "Belangrijk:" or "Let op:" or just a sentence.

### Structural rules
- 1 H1 (the title, set by Next.js metadata)
- 4-6 H2 sections (not 1 like the neighborhood blog audit caught)

- 5-8 internal links to `/nl/voor-trainers`, `/nl/studio-huren`, `/nl/word-trainer`, the relevant sub-pages, AND 2-3 other supply-side blog posts (cluster reinforcement)
- 1 outbound authoritative link (Belastingdienst, KvK, ANW, etc.) where relevant for E-E-A-T

- Author byline (P.M. de Vries — already in Org schema)
- Date published + last modified
- BlogPostingJsonLd + BreadcrumbJsonLd via `@/components/seo/json-ld`
